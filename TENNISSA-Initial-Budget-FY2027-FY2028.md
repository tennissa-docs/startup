## Notes

### Rules built into this budget

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Separate funds** | Section 25F gifts are held in a separate account and never used for salaries beyond the permitted admin share (FIN-001 §6, §11). |
| **90% rule** | At least 90% of Section 25F income must fund scholarships for eligible students within the required period. The admin share is capped at 10% and must cover processing fees. |
| **Credit-eligible gifts** | Only cash gifts from individuals, made on or after Jan 1, 2027, after TDOE listing and IRS SGO registration. Corporate, noncash and event revenue go to the Operating Fund. |
| **Startup costs** | Costs before Jan 2027 (charter, Form 1023, legal, platform setup, insurance) must be paid from founder or community operating gifts, not 25F funds. |
| **Compensation** | No revenue-based or percentage pay. Any Executive Director pay is a fixed amount approved by disinterested directors (COI-001 §11). |

### Items that need quotes
